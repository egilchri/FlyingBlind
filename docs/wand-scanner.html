FlyingBlind · no-solder wand

The wand scanner

A lipstick-size power bank becomes a handle you hold like a flashlight. A plastic ruler on top carries the same sensor, board and buzzer as the first version. Everything is held on with foam tape and two Velcro straps. Point it and sweep.

Parts, compared with the first version

  • Same: TF-Luna sensor, ESP32 DevKit, buzzer module, jumper wires and foam tape. The wiring diagram and firmware don't change.
  • Swap the power bank for a lipstick-style one, about 2.5 cm across and 9.5 cm long (2,600 mAh is common).
  • Add a 15 cm plastic ruler as the spine, or any stiff strip about 2.5 cm wide.
  • Add two Velcro cable straps to hold the power bank under the ruler.
  • Add a short USB cable (about 20 cm) that fits both the power bank's output and the board.

Trade-offs

  • Plugs together like the first version, and it's easy to take apart and change.
  • A natural flashlight grip: the arm, the wand and the beam all point the same way, which suits sweeping.
  • Longer than the matchbox design, with the electronics out in the open, so they can snag.
  • The buzzer hangs under the ruler, so the tone comes from below the beam.
  • This is a 3D model at true size, not a photo. Battery life and weight are estimates.
